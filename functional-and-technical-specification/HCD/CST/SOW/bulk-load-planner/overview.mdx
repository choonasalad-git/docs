## Objective

Provide users with visibility of the estimated physical load of a Sales Order during order entry, without requiring a shipment to first be created.

Packaging and bulk values will be available at individual Sales Order line level, with consolidated load totals available from the Shipping tab.

## Problem statement

Sales Orders are currently raised without sufficient visibility of shipping and load requirements, particularly when entering bulk orders.

Users may need to create a shipment before they can determine the approximate physical size of an order. This makes it difficult to assess pallet, carton, and transport requirements during initial order entry.

This can result in:

- Orders exceeding expected pallet or vehicle capacity.
- Inefficient transport and warehouse planning.
- Shipments being created solely to determine approximate load size.
- Additional administration where orders need to be changed after shipment creation.
- Delays in identifying unusually large or incorrectly entered order quantities.

## Proposed solution

Introduce packaging configuration against the Stock Item and expose the relevant bulk information directly within Sales Order entry.

Each Sales Order line will display calculated packaging information based on the ordered quantity and the packaging configuration of the selected stock item.

At the order level, a **Load Summary** panel will be added to the Shipping tab. This panel will provide consolidated carton, pallet, and other relevant load totals for the entire Sales Order.

To avoid unnecessary processing during normal order entry, order-level totals will not continuously recalculate. A **Calculate Load** button will allow the user to explicitly request calculation of the current order's load.
