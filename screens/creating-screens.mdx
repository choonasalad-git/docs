---
title: "Create a screen"
description: "Build and register a custom Acumatica screen."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the graph">Define the primary view, supporting views, and actions.</Step>
  <Step title="Create the screen">Bind the form and grids to graph views and DAC fields.</Step>
  <Step title="Assign a screen ID">Use the numbering convention established for your tenant or product.</Step>
  <Step title="Add navigation">Place the screen in the correct workspace and category.</Step>
  <Step title="Test access">Verify roles, rights, keyboard flow, and error states.</Step>
</Steps>
